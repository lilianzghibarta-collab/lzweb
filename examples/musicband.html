About Us

The Sound of Tomorrow

Electric Dreams emerged from the underground Los Angeles music scene in 2018, fusing classic rock energy with electronic innovation to create a sound that's both nostalgic and futuristic.

With three platinum albums, sold-out world tours, and millions of streams, we've established ourselves as pioneers of a new musical movement that refuses to be confined by genre boundaries.

Our live performances are legendary for their energy, visual spectacle, and the raw connection we create with our audience. This isn't just music—it's an experience.
