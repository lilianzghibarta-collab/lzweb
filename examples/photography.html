About

I'm Alexander Hart, a fine art and commercial photographer based in New York City. For over a decade, I've been dedicated to capturing the essence of life through my lens.

My work spans fashion, portraiture, and editorial photography, with a focus on creating timeless images that tell compelling stories. Each photograph is crafted with attention to light, composition, and emotion.

My portfolio has been featured in Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, and The New York Times. I work with both emerging and established brands to create visual narratives that resonate.
